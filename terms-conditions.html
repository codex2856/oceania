Terms & Conditions

Last updated: March 2026

1. Booking confirmation

A 50% deposit of the total cost is required to secure an appointment. The booking is only confirmed once the deposit is received.

2. Cancellations

Payments made are non-refundable, because the time slot is blocked in the studio’s schedule.

3. Rescheduling

You can reschedule once, as long as you ask at least 72 hours in advance. The new date must fall within 30 days of the original booking and depends on availability.

4. Late arrivals

There is a maximum grace period of 30 minutes. After that, the appointment may be canceled or shortened depending on availability, and the deposit is forfeited.

5. No-shows

If you do not attend without notice, the service is considered used, with no rescheduling or refund.

6. Makeup results

No refunds are given for personal preferences about the final result. Reasonable adjustments can be made during the appointment.

7. Services outside the studio

On-location services may carry an additional travel fee, depending on distance and preparation time.

8. Photography and content

The studio may photograph or record the finished work for its portfolio, social media, or promotional purposes. If you prefer not to appear, tell us before we begin.

9. Weddings, events, and group bookings

Additional conditions may apply, such as higher deposits, specific preparation times, or custom agreements.

10. Changes by Oceania Studios

If the studio has to change or cancel an appointment due to unforeseen circumstances, you can reschedule or receive a full refund of your deposit. The studio also reserves the right to update these terms.
