Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer, and how do I ask about prices?

Makeup services, beauty coaching, and private classes are listed on the Services page. Message us and we’ll prepare a personalized quote.

How do I book?

A deposit is required to secure your date (40% of the total cost on the current site; the Terms say 50%, see README). The rest is paid on the day of the appointment.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, if you ask within the same month and the new date is available. Deposits are non-refundable for cancellations or no-shows.

Do you offer on-location makeup?

Yes, at your home or at the event venue (events, photo shoots, and weddings) anywhere in the Netherlands. Travel costs may apply.

How to get here

Parking is available at the back, and there are bike racks at the entrance.
