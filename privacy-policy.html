Privacy Policy

Oceania Studios is committed to protecting the personal data and the right to privacy of everyone who contacts us.

Information we collect

We collect personal information you provide voluntarily when you register, show interest in our services, or contact us. This may include your name and contact details (email, address, phone number), login credentials such as passwords, and payment data such as card number and security code if you make a purchase.

How we use your information

We use your information to create accounts, send administrative information, manage orders and services, respond to inquiries, send marketing communications, and for other business purposes. We rely on legitimate interests, performance of a contract, consent, and legal obligations.

Sharing your information

We only share your information with your consent, to comply with the law, to provide services, to protect our rights, or to fulfill business obligations.

Security of your information

We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, but no measure is completely foolproof.

Retention of your information

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes described here, unless the law requires a longer period.

Your privacy rights

In regions such as the European Economic Area, you may request access to, a copy of, rectification, erasure, or restriction of the processing of your data and, where applicable, data portability.
