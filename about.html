Who I am?

I’m Yoseany Parra, professional makeup artist and Ho’oponopono meditation guide.

Oceania Studios was created to merge beauty and balance — makeup as a bridge between the outer and the inner world.

My Vision

My goal goes beyond aesthetics: I want every person who sits in my chair to leave with more peace, confidence, and emotional balance than they arrived with.

Since 2015, my career has been built through dedication, practice, and continuous learning.

Trained at Colombia MakeUp School and Glow Makeup Academy in Medellín, with specialization in blending and armored skin techniques with Ferzeta Academy.

Makeup Artistry

Specialized in blending and armored skin, with experience in natural, glam, and editorial looks.

Beauty Education

Personalized training and self-makeup courses focused on confidence, authenticity, and emotional well-being.

Creative Direction

Visual storytelling through makeup and photography, with brand collaborations and artistic projects.
